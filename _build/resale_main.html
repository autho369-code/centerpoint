Everything a Chicago closing asks for.

A resale request is rarely one document. Lenders, title companies, and buyer attorneys each work from their own checklist, so we prepare the full set.

Section 22.1 disclosure packet

The statutory disclosure a condominium association provides on a proper request from a selling owner: finances, governing documents, insurance, and known obligations affecting the unit.

Paid assessment letter

Written confirmation of the unit account: current balance, assessment amount, any special assessment levied or pending, and amounts due at closing.

Lender questionnaire

The association-level questions a lender asks before financing: insurance, litigation, delinquencies, reserves, owner occupancy, and commercial space.

Certificate of insurance

Evidence of the association's master policy, issued to the lender or title company as the closing file requires.

Governing documents

The recorded declaration with every amendment, the bylaws, and current rules. The version that actually governs, not a copy from a prior sale.

Project eligibility questionnaires

Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac condominium project questionnaires, which often decide whether a buyer's financing clears underwriting.

Section 22.1 does not apply to every association.

Section 22.1 is part of the Illinois Condominium Property Act, which governs condominiums. Many Illinois HOAs and townhome associations fall under the Common Interest Community Association Act instead, a separate statute with its own disclosure and records rules.

When a "22.1" request arrives for an association governed by CICAA, we respond with what that statute and the recorded documents actually require. We confirm the governing statute before drafting anything, and we refer anything involving pending litigation to association counsel.

Common questions.

What is a Section 22.1 disclosure?

Section 22.1 of the Illinois Condominium Property Act sets what a condominium association must disclose when an owner sells a unit. On a proper request from the seller or their agent, the association provides information about its finances, governing documents, insurance, and known obligations affecting the unit.

Does Section 22.1 apply to an HOA or townhome association?

Often not. Many Illinois HOAs and townhome associations are governed by the Common Interest Community Association Act, which has its own disclosure and records provisions. The association's counsel should confirm which statute applies.

What documents does a closing usually require?

Most Chicago closings need some combination of the disclosure packet, a paid assessment letter, a lender questionnaire, a certificate of insurance, and current governing documents with all recorded amendments. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac project questionnaires are requested more and more often.

Who can request resale documents?

The selling owner, or someone acting for them, usually their attorney or listing agent. Lenders and title companies typically request the questionnaire and insurance certificate directly.
